Theorem. lim_{x→2} x^2 = 4.
Proof. Let ε > 0. Since |x^2 − 4| = |x − 2|·|x + 2|, and |x − 2| < 1 gives
|x + 2| < 5, choose δ = min{1, \frac{ε}{5}}.
Then 0 < |x − 2| < δ  ⇒  |x^2 − 4| < 5δ ≤ ε.   ∎
